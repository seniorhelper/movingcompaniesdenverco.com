How requests are handled

This site is a free referral service from Eye To Ad Media in Denver. It is not a moving company and does not move, store or insure anything.

1. You send a short request

Name, phone, and the basics of your move. Contact details are required so a mover can answer; everything else helps them quote accurately.

2. A person looks at it

We read each request to make sure it is real and to see which type of mover suits it: local, long-distance or business.

3. It goes to no more than three companies

We send the request only to movers that meet our standard: an active Colorado PUC household goods permit (plus the USDOT number and matching federal authority for interstate jobs), insurance in force, and an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau. We check each one ourselves before it receives a request, and we may stop sending to one that falls short later.

4. They contact you; you decide

The companies may call or text you, within the consent you gave. You compare their written estimates. You owe nothing to us or to any mover unless you sign a contract with one.

What we do not do

  • We do not publish rankings, stars or names on this site, because our standard is about checks you can repeat, not a popularity score.
  • We do not guarantee any mover's work. Partner movers are independent businesses and are responsible for their own service.
  • We do not ask you to pay us anything.

Run the same checks on anyone who contacts you. The ten-minute checklist works on every company.

What each check on our standard means

Permit. The state PUC permit shows the company is allowed to carry household goods within Colorado. The federal USDOT number plays that role for jobs that cross a state line.

Insurance. We want proof that cover is in force, because uninsured damage is the loss a customer feels most. We ask to see the certificate, and so should you.

A+ rating. The Better Business Bureau grade is only one signal. We use it as a floor, not as proof of quality, and we read complaint patterns where they exist.

When you are ready, send a request from the home page.
