Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026. This website is run by Eye To Ad Media of Denver, Colorado ("we", "us").

What we receive

Only what you type into the request form: your name, phone number, optional email, an approximate ZIP code and destination, the size and month of your move, and your consent checkbox. We also log the page you used, the time, the version of the terms shown, and the wording of the consent you agreed to, so we can show what you agreed to. Our spam traps record a timer value and an empty hidden field. This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

Why we use it

  • To review your request and send it to no more than three moving companies that serve your area.
  • To contact you ourselves about the request.
  • To prevent abuse and to keep the records the law expects of us.

Who sees it

The movers we send your request to, and the service providers that host the site and handle our mail. We do not sell your details to advertisers or data brokers. Each mover is an independent company with its own privacy practices and is expected to use your details only for the move you asked about.

Calls and texts

If you ticked the consent box, we and those movers may contact you by phone, text or email, including by autodialed or prerecorded means. Consent is not a condition of buying anything. Reply STOP to any text to end texting, and tell any caller to stop calling.

How long we keep it

Requests are kept while we need them to respond and to keep consent records, and then deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

Email zach@eyetoad.com to see, correct or delete what we hold, or to opt out of contact. Colorado residents can use the rights the Colorado Privacy Act provides. We will tell the movers who received your request to stop contacting you.

Security and children

The site is served over HTTPS and the form posts only to our own server. It is for adults planning a move and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new date.

Denver Moving Companies Field Guide is a free referral service operated by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado. We are not a moving company, and we do not move anything.
